# Data Access Policy

## PII Access
- Agents may not access Personally Identifiable Information (PII) without explicit authorization.
- PII includes: name, email, phone, address, payment details.

## Database Access
- Read access to customer records requires a logged justification.
- Write access to customer records requires human approval for batches over 100 records.

## Retention
- All data access events must be logged and retained for 90 days minimum.
