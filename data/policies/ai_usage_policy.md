# AI Usage Policy

## Approved Use Cases
- Customer support query answering
- Automated refund processing within approved limits
- Inventory and order status lookups

## Prohibited Actions
- AI agents may not send external emails without human review.
- AI agents may not modify pricing data directly.
- AI agents may not access systems outside their defined tool scope.

## Risk Thresholds
- Risk score 0–39: Auto-approve and execute.
- Risk score 40–69: Log and monitor, execute with warning.
- Risk score 70–100: Pause and route to human for approval.
