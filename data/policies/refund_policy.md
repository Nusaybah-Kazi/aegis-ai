# Refund Policy

## Automated Refund Limits
- Refunds up to ₹5,000 may be processed automatically by the Refund Agent.
- Refunds between ₹5,001 and ₹25,000 require manager approval before processing.
- Refunds above ₹25,000 require senior management approval and a written justification.

## Eligibility
- Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.
- Digital products are non-refundable after download.

## Audit
- Every refund action must be logged with agent ID, amount, timestamp, and approver.
